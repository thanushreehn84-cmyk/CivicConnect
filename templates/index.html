HOW IT WORKS

From Complaint to Resolution

The process is designed to be simple and transparent.

01

Submit Complaint

Describe the civic issue and provide its location and relevant evidence.

02

Receive Complaint ID

A unique complaint ID is generated after successful submission.

03

Department Review

An administrator reviews the complaint and forwards it to the relevant department.

04

Track Status

Citizens can use their complaint ID to monitor the progress of their complaint.

ABOUT CIVICCONNECT

Building a More Responsive Civic System

CivicConnect is a digital platform designed to make civic issue reporting easier and more accessible.

Citizens can report problems such as illegal construction, environmental issues, traffic problems, and public safety concerns.

Administrators can review submitted complaints, assign them to the appropriate department, and update their status.

Supported Complaint Categories

  • Illegal Construction
  • Environmental Issues
  • Traffic Issues
  • Public Safety
  • Other Civic Issues
